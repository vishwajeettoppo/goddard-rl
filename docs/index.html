What changed, and why

  1. R0 → R1: boundary-mode Beta head. The unimodal head collapses to a deterministic throttle early; the boundary head keeps mass near 0 and 1, which is where the optimal bang–singular–coast policy lives.
  2. R2: burnout features and a one-sided early-burnout penalty stop the agent burning propellant in the thickest air.
  3. R3: behaviour cloning from the singular-arc law gives a strong start but suppresses exploration.
  4. R4: residual action around the singular arc bakes the structure in and is the largest single gain.
  5. R5: potential-based shaping with a terminal-zero potential is neutral here (76.8%) because the altitude reward is already dense.
  6. R6: an actor-side max-Q cap plus mode-based evaluation removes the Beta train/eval mismatch.
