The constrained Goddard rocket:
why a baseline PPO agent stalls, and what fixes it

Maximise apogee under a dynamic-pressure limit with RL. We reproduce the paper's environment and baselines, retrain the ablation ladder, and add a residual action that closes most of the gap to the best non-RL baseline.

1 · Problem

Throttle is easy, the constraint is not

  • State (h, v, m); thrust T = u·Tmax.
  • Drag grows with ρ(h)·v², so climbing early is expensive.
  • q = ½ρv² <= Qmax caps speed low in the atmosphere.
  • The optimal policy is bang–singular–coast: burn, ride the singular arc, coast.

The paper's premise: RL fails here because the problem is underspecified for a unimodal policy, not because the reward is wrong.

  • 18 held-out scenarios: 3 fuel loads × 6 Qmax values.
  • Domain randomisation is wider than the grid, and evaluation pairs are excluded.
  • Reward: Δh / 1000, γ = 1 → telescopes to apogee.
  • Episodes end at apogee or 200 s.
2 · Evaluator

One integrator for every method

  • RK4 on (h, v, m), dt = 0.1 s.
  • Step split exactly at fuel exhaustion.
  • If the nominal step violates Qmax, binary-search the largest feasible thrust; clamped steps pay a 5% drag surcharge.
  • Numba-parallel version steps 256 environments in ~0.14 ms.

3 · Failure demo

Unimodal Beta collapses; boundary Beta does not

  • R0 (ε = 1) is forced to put mass near its mean — it cannot represent the two-sided bang-bang policy.
  • R1 (ε = 0.01) keeps mass at 0 and 1, so the same PPO update can sustain exploration at the boundary.
  • BC warm start (R3) drives entropy down immediately: strong start, weak exploration.

6 · Takeaways

Limitations and what we would do next

  • The paper does not specify t_switch for the unconstrained optimum; three heavy/tight rows remain irreproducible and are reported as-is.
  • t_target = 75x − 8x² turns over at 4687 kg and is negative beyond 9375 kg, inside the training distribution. We provide a monotone replacement and audit it.
  • Two seeds are not enough to claim statistical significance; the spread is plotted.
  • Next: a deterministic actor (SAC/TD3) with a squashed Gaussian, and a direct collocation optimum at dt = 0.1 for a tighter upper bound.
